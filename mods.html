Capybaras+

Capybaras brings the world’s chillest animal to Minecraft. They spawn in swamps and mangrove biomes and can be tamed with sugar cane. Once tamed, you can give them fun cosmetics like a top hat or equip them with iron, gold, or diamond armor. There are plenty of different variants of capybaras to discover, making each one feel unique. When killing them, they drop capybara fur blocks that can be crafted into carpets.
