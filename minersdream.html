Miners Dream

Overview

Welcome to the revival of an old favorite! Miners Dream brings back the iconic tool from the legendary OreSpawn mod, now updated and polished for modern Minecraft versions.

Whether you're a veteran Minecrafter who remembers the good old days or a newcomer looking for a powerful mining tool, this mod has you covered. The Miners Dream is the ultimate mining companion, designed to make resource gathering faster, more efficient, and more rewarding than ever before.

Key Features

Ultimate Mining Tool

The Miners Dream tool mines blocks in a 5x5x15 area, making resource gathering incredibly fast and efficient.

Mining Blocks

The Miners Dream tool will not break any ores vanilla or modded, it only breaks vanilla blocks.

Cheap Crafting Recipe

You get FOUR miners dreams per crafting so you can easily get your resources back by using them!

Usage Guide

Crafting Recipe

The Miners Dream can be crafted using a combination of diamond, obsidian, and other rare materials. The exact recipe can be found in the recipe book or JEI/REI if installed.

Mining Mechanics

  • Mines over 5x5x15!!
  • Works with mining all vanilla blocks
  • Easily find ores this way
  • Will not break any modded ores

Tips & Tricks

  • Great for strip-mining and large excavation projects
  • Use JEI to learn how to craft the Miners Dreamed!
