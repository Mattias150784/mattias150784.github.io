Overview

Miners Dream brings back the famous mining item from the old OreSpawn mod, now updated for modern Minecraft. Miners Dream is the ultimate resource gathering tool.

Key Features

⛏️

5×5×15 Mining Area

Mines a massive volume of blocks in a single swing strip mining has never been faster.

💎

Ore Safe

Will not break any ores (vanilla or modded) only clears stone and dirt, leaving all ores exposed.

🔨

Cheap to Craft

Each craft gives you FOUR Miners Dreams, so replacing them is never an issue.

Usage Tips

  • Best used for large scale strip mining and excavation
  • Use JEI to look up the crafting recipe in game
  • Ores are left untouched, so you can easily spot them after clearing stone
  • Great for clearing space for underground bases
