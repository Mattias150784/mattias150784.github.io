Color Expansion

Overview

Color Expansion was a simple yet effective mod that brought vibrant new colors to Minecraft's building palette. This mod added a variety of colorful block variants, allowing builders to create more diverse and visually appealing structures.

With straightforward crafting recipes and compatibility with the latest Minecraft versions at the time, Color Expansion made it easy to enhance your builds with a rainbow of options.

📦 Total Blocks Added: 32 Blocks

Features

  • 32 Total Blocks: 16 colored brick variants + 16 colored stone brick variants
  • Simple Crafting: Easy to remember recipes for all block types
  • Compatible: Worked with the latest Minecraft versions at the time of release
  • Vibrant Colors: Full spectrum of colors for creative building
