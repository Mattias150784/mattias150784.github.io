Overview

Pixel Blocks combines lucky blocks and pixelmon together to create a unique and chaos experience. You can break a Pixel Block and get a Weedle or break it and get a Mewtwo! Great for challenge maps, randomizer runs, and multiplayer events.

This mod is no longer maintained and will not receive compatibility updates.

How It Worked

01

Place a Pixel Block

Obtain and place a Pixel Block anywhere in the world.

02

Break It

Break the block a random Pokémon spawns at its location.

03

Fight or Catch

Fight the Pokémon that appears could be anything!
