Mysti Grecia

Overview

Mysti Grecia was a Minecraft mod that brought the world of Greek mythology and Roman mythology to life. Getting inspiration from the Percy Jackson franchise, the mod integrated mythical creatures.

The mod was designed to create a mythological adventure, allowing players to encounter greek mythology monsters, craft amazing weapons, and even more was planned!

Key Features

Mythical Creatures

Encounter mythical creatures from Greek mythology including Centaurs, Sea Serpents, and Satyrs.

Celestial Weapons

Craft unique weapons such as Riptide, Ivlis, Greek Aspis, Backbitter

Ores

Ores like Bronze, Silver, Tin, and Celestial Bronze!

Creatures

  • Centaur: Half horse half human hybrid, if hit will shoot arrows at you with it's bow.
  • Satyr: Half goat half human peaceful and would never attack on death would drop a goat horn and wool.
  • Sea Serpent: Roamed the oceans and scary to come across if you do slay the Serpent you are rewarded with it's scales to make armor with them.
  • Stymphalian Bird: Bronze feathered bird would shoot it's feathers at anyone or anything if you could defeat it you could get some of it's feathers to make arrows.

Community

The Mysti Grecia community was where players regularly shared their ideas, showcased impressive builds, their own artwork and mods, or asking for help with any bugs with the mod!
