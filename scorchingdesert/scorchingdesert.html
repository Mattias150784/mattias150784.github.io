Scorching Desert

A Minecraft mod focused on expanding on Minecraft's boring Desert Biome and bringing life to it!

Overview

Scorching Desert is a Minecraft mod focused on expanding on the vanilla desert, adding new mobs, blocks, structures, and biomes!

This page is the official Scorching Desert Wiki. Search around for anything you are looking we even have specific categories such as Mobs, Blocks, Items, Tools & Gear, Structures, and Features.

Getting Started

Scorching Desert

Start up your Minecraft world and find a Desert or Mesa! You might notice there may be some new type of wildlife living in there. But be careful because not all the animals are peaceful.

Mobs

This section lists all mobs introduced by Scorching Desert.

Coyote

Listen to Coyote Howl

Passive during the day (unless hit), Hostile when nighttime,

  • Type: Hostile
  • Spawn Biomes: Desert, Mesa
  • Health: 8 hearts
  • Attack: 3 Attack Damage
  • Drops: Coyote Dagger

Road Runner

Passive, always runs away from almost everything, really fast

  • Type: Passive
  • Spawn Biomes: Desert, Mesa
  • Health: 4 hearts
  • Attack: 0 Attack Damage
  • Drops: Road Runner Meat, Road Runner Feather

Items

This section lists all items introduced by Scorching Desert.

Consumables

  • [Road Runner Meat] – restores as much hunger as chicken
  • [Road Runner Cooked Meat] – restores as much hunger as cooked chicken

Miscellaneous Items

  • [Road Runner Egg] – randomly popped out from the Road Runner
  • [Road Runner Feather] – upon death Road Runner has a chance to drop
  • [Coyote Fang] – upon death Coyote has a chance to drop

Structures

This section lists all structures introduced by Scorching Desert.

[Sunken Temple]

  • Location: Below the sand in the Desert.
  • Loot: Ancient Dagger
  • Traps: Place any blocks in the parkour, TNT will ignite and dripstone will fall, making you die.

[Sunken Red Temple]

  • Location: Below the red sand in the Red Desert.
  • Loot: Ancient Dagger
  • Traps: Place any blocks in the parkour, TNT will ignite and dripstone will fall, making you die.
