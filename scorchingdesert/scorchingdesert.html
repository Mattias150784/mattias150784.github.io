Scorching Desert

A Minecraft mod that expands on the vanilla Desert biome, adding new mobs, blocks, structures, and features to bring the desert to life.

Overview

Scorching Desert expands on the vanilla Minecraft desert with new mobs, blocks, structures, and world generation. Start a world and head to a Desert or Mesa biome to find new wildlife, dangers, and things to discover.

Not everything you find will be friendly. Explore carefully.

Stabbing Enchantment

A dagger exclusive enchantment. On hit, there is a 25% chance to deal bonus damage scaling with the dagger tier, dealing an additional 6 damage based on what material the dagger is made from.

Structures

Sunken Temple

  • Location Buried below sand in the Desert biome.
  • Loot Ancient Dagger
  • Traps Placing any blocks in the parkour section will ignite TNT and cause dripstone to fall.

Sunken Red Temple

  • Location Buried below red sand in the Badlands and Mesa biomes.
  • Loot Ancient Dagger
  • Traps Placing any blocks in the parkour section will ignite TNT and cause dripstone to fall.

Features and Mechanics

Quicksand Lake

Watch your step in Desert biomes. Quicksand Lakes can generate naturally and will trap and slowly suffocate anything that steps in them.

Red Quicksand Lake

The same danger as Quicksand Lakes, but found in the Badlands. Watch your step around Mesa biomes.
