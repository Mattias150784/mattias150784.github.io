Pedestals

Overview

Pedestals is a decorative mod that adds functional and visually appealing pedestals to Minecraft. These pedestals allow players to display their items in style, with smooth animations that bring your treasures to life.

Perfect for museums, trophy rooms, or any build where you want to showcase your rare finds, Pedestals transforms ordinary item storage into an elegant display solution. Items gently float and rotate above the pedestal, creating a magical effect that enhances any room.

Key Features

  • 🎨 Beautiful Displays: Items hover and rotate above pedestals with smooth animations
  • 🧱 Multiple Variants: Different pedestal designs to match your build style
  • 🔄 Easy to Use: Simply place an item on a pedestal to display it
  • 📦 Item Storage: Pedestals can store items when not in display mode
  • 🎮 Multiplayer Friendly: Works seamlessly on servers
  • 🔌 Mod Compatible: Displays items from any mod

Usage Guide

Basic Usage

  1. Craft a pedestal using the recipes shown in game
  2. Place the pedestal in your world
  3. Right-click the pedestal with any item to display it
  4. Right-click again to retrieve the item

Display Tips

  • Use pedestals to create museum exhibits
  • Display rare items in your trophy room
  • Create themed collections (e.g., all ores, all tools)
  • Use different pedestal types to match your build style
  • Combine with item frames for more complex displays

Building Ideas

Here are some creative ways to use Pedestals in your builds:

🏛️ Museum Exhibit

Create a dedicated museum building with different rooms for various item categories. Use pedestals to display rare mob drops, ores, tools, and decorative items.

🏆 Trophy Room

Design a personal trophy room to showcase your greatest achievements. Display rare items like dragon eggs, nether stars, and enchanted gear on pedestals.

🛍️ Shop Displays

Use pedestals in your shops to display items for sale. The floating animation draws attention to your merchandise.

🎨 Art Gallery

Create an art gallery using item frames and pedestals. Display paintings, heads, and decorative blocks in an elegant setting.

🏰 Throne Room

Enhance your throne room with pedestals displaying royal treasures and artifacts around your throne.
