Overview

Capybaras brings the world's chillest animal to Minecraft. They can be found in swamp and mangrove biomes, and with a few sugar cane you can make one your own. Once tamed, you can equip them with cosmetics like a top hat, or protect them with iron, gold, or diamond armor.

With over 10 different capybara variants to find, each one feels a little different. When slain they drop capybara fur blocks that can be crafted into carpets, you can also shear the capybara to get the fur block.

Cosmetics and capybara armor can be found inside chests scattered around the world, so make sure to explore.

Taming

Capybaras are passive mobs that roam swamp and mangrove biomes. They will never attack you.

01

Find a Capybara

Head to a Swamp or Mangrove biome. Capybaras can usually be found hanging in or around water.

02

Bring Sugar Cane

Sugar cane is the only thing a capybara can be tamed with. Grow or find some before you go looking for one.

03

Right Click to Feed

Hold sugar cane and right click the capybara to feed it. You may need to do this multiple times before it becomes tamed.

04

Tamed

Once tamed, the capybara will follow you. You can now equip it with cosmetics and armor or make it sit.

Cosmetics

Once your capybara is tamed, you can equip it with cosmetics. These are purely decorative and found inside chests scattered around the world.

Top Hat

A fancy top hat for your capybara. Found in chests around the world.

More cosmetics can be found by exploring and looting chests. Keep an eye out while adventuring.

Armor

Protect your tamed capybara with armor. There are three tiers available, each offering different levels of protection. Capybara armor is found in chests around the world, just like cosmetics.

Tier Material Where to Find
Iron Iron Capybara Armor Chests around the world
Gold Golden Capybara Armor Chests around the world
Diamond Diamond Capybara Armor Chests around the world
